# Single remaining user input for John 9

The original 1919 Traditional Chinese Union Version (和合本), 神版 identity is
fixed and must not be asked again. Lightweight production is also fixed. The
only remaining user input is one combined source-review bundle:

1. One stable online/source artifact covering John 9:1-41, with URL or locator,
   provider, exact captured bytes, and SHA-256.
2. One designated human reviewer with a stable name or ID and a dated approval
   of those bytes, the 41-verse order, the six-Beat outline, the unnamed
   observer role, the immutable final state, and contextual wording.
3. The source's displayed reuse/public-domain note, or a user confirmation that
   use is limited to the private small-game scope while any source images remain
   private.

This is one approval package, not three separate decision rounds. One reviewer
may cover text, story order, player limits, and sensitivity for this small game.
Cambridge scans, institutional provenance, double-key transcription, a third
adjudicator, and multiple specialist reviewers are not default requirements.
They are escalation options only if a concrete variant/glyph dispute,
conflicting source, unresolved reuse issue, or public/commercial distribution
risk appears.

The artifact must identify the fixed original 1919 和合本 神版. It must not be
silently replaced with 1988 New Punctuation CUV/CUNP, 2010 RCUV, modern or
legacy CCB, YouVersion/API text, or eBible `cmn-cu89t`.

Until this bundle is supplied, `scripture.availability` remains `missing`, all
41 `exactText` values remain null, and the Gate remains
`blocked-text-unavailable`. Revised Foundation policy coordination is a
platform dependency, not another user content decision. Runtime, map, art, and
audio work remains prohibited until that coordination is complete.
